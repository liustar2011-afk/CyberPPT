# 视觉风格09：纯白 + 深蓝领导汇报｜多页面适配固化版

## 01｜色彩与基础气质

Palette: white #FFFFFF, deep blue #12355B, muted amber #D9772B, title #12355B, body #303030, secondary #6F7275, divider #C9CDD1.

Use deep blue for primary relationships, core judgments, key structural carriers and major conclusions. Reserve muted amber only for genuine risks, exceptions, constraints, pending status and occasional decision-critical emphasis; never use it as a category color or decorative accent.

Create a high-end senior leadership briefing page in a structured editorial business-infographic style: authoritative, calm, polished, precise and content-led. The page should feel like a refined executive-report infographic rather than a dashboard, poster, UI screen or icon-based diagram.

---

## 02｜固定视觉 DNA

Keep the visual language consistent across all page types while allowing the page skeleton to change with semantics.

Preserve the following visual DNA:

- pure-white background with restrained deep-blue hierarchy;
- one visually dominant focus and one clear reading path;
- concise Chinese presentation text with clear hierarchy;
- semantic visual materials integrated with the information structure when useful;
- generous white space, precise alignment and thin pale-blue structural relationships;
- few structural components and minimal decorative graphics;
- editorial, content-led composition rather than dashboard-like or template-like layout.

Prefer asymmetric and unequally weighted composition when it strengthens the page logic; do not impose asymmetry when the source genuinely requires balanced comparison or equal weighting.

Uniform visual language does not mean uniform page skeleton or uniform visual-material type.

---

## 03｜页面语义优先原则

Derive the page structure from the dominant semantic relationship in the source content.

Identify the primary relationship before composing:

- center-to-periphery → radial / hub-and-spoke;
- sequence or flow → main chain / process;
- time → timeline / roadmap;
- cause and effect → causal expansion;
- comparison → comparative structure;
- composition or dependency → main body + capability clusters;
- judgment and proof → conclusion + evidence;
- real-world business relationship → scene / object-led composition;
- quantitative argument → key metric + trend / evidence;
- governance or responsibility → relationship axis + boundary / support structure.

Do not force a central, radial, ring-based, card-based or other familiar layout onto content whose semantics require another structure.

Use only the number of semantic regions required by the content. Prefer fewer, clearly differentiated regions over repeated modules.

---

## 04｜文字与信息组织

Rewrite the supplied source copy into concise, conclusion-first Chinese presentation text.

Condense for presentation while preserving all material business meaning, including facts, numbers, conditions, scope, responsibilities, status and claim strength. Do not invent unsupported facts, slogans, conclusions or policy language.

Place text close to the related business object, visual material or structural carrier so each local area forms one coherent information unit.

Prefer short conclusion-first paragraphs and compact local explanations. Use bullets only when parallel items genuinely need distinction.

Do not reduce complete business meaning into disconnected keywords or labels merely to simplify the layout.

The page should remain understandable as a reading-type PPT without oral explanation.

---

## 05｜视觉对象与场景

Choose visual expression according to page semantics. Visual materials may include scenes, business objects, equipment, facilities, documents, resources, data graphics or structural relationships; realistic scenes are optional.

Derive visual subjects from the specific business objects, actors, actions, resources and outcomes in the source content. Do not map broad content categories to fixed scene types.

Use visual materials only when they strengthen business context, evidence or understanding. Otherwise let text, structure or data relationships carry the page.

Every visual element must support a specific topic, judgment, indicator, stakeholder, evidence point or business relationship. Avoid generic industry imagery used only to signal “power industry” or “technology”.

People are optional. If present, keep them non-portrait, visually subordinate and relevant to the business meaning.

---

## 06｜结构载体与空间层级

Establish one strong semantic and visual anchor on each page. It may be a conclusion, key business object, metric, relationship, mechanism, stage, visual object or risk state.

Create visible hierarchy through scale, position, contrast and spacing rather than additional boxes or decoration.

Supporting information must remain subordinate while fully readable.

Use simple, restrained structural carriers only when they help explain the page logic. Keep depth subtle and matte.

Avoid glossy 3D, sci-fi platforms, excessive perspective, floating objects or game-like visual effects.

---

## 07｜连接关系与精细度

Use thin, sparse and purposeful connectors. Use deep blue for primary relationships and pale blue for secondary relationships.

Connectors must explain direction, dependency, flow or boundary rather than decorate empty space.

Use white space, precise alignment and restrained dividers to separate semantic layers. Avoid thick borders and heavy framing.

Text blocks, visual objects, connectors and structural carriers should align to a consistent editorial grid.

---

# 08｜页面构图原型库

Select an archetype according to the dominant semantic relationship rather than the page title. These are options, not templates.

## 8.1 中心放射式｜定位 / 架构 / 生态 / 多主体关系

Use only when the source has a genuine center-to-periphery relationship.

Place the core object at or near the center, major directions or mechanisms around it, and stakeholders, outcomes or application domains farther outward as required.

Use restrained radial relationships. Do not use this archetype when the content lacks a real center.

---

## 8.2 结论证据式｜背景 / 必要性 / 核心判断 / 价值说明

Use when one judgment is supported by several facts, business realities or quantitative proofs.

Let the conclusion dominate; arrange supporting evidence in fewer, unequally weighted regions. Add visual evidence only where it materially strengthens the argument.

Avoid turning every evidence point into an equal card.

---

## 8.3 主链流程式｜业务流程 / 数据流通 / 实施机制 / 服务闭环

Use when the source has a clear sequence, flow or closed loop.

Build one visible main chain with meaningful stages and emphasize only the critical nodes or final business result.

Use directional arrows sparingly. Avoid rows of identical process boxes.

---

## 8.4 时间路线式｜阶段建设 / 路线图 / 年度计划 / 演进路径

Use when the sequence is explicitly temporal.

Build one clear time carrier, place milestones and deliverables close to their relevant stage, and emphasize the current or target stage when appropriate.

Avoid generic equal-node timelines.

---

## 8.5 场景 / 对象主导式｜应用场景 / 客户案例 / 业务方案 / 场景共建

Use when a business environment, key object or operating relationship is the most direct carrier of meaning.

Let the visual carrier form the page skeleton, with actors, inputs, actions and outputs annotated directly around or within it.

Use only a few connectors. Do not surround the visual carrier with unrelated cards.

---

## 8.6 因果分析式｜问题 / 原因 / 约束 / 风险传导

Use when the source explains why an issue occurs or how conditions lead to an outcome.

Show the main problem, consequence or constraint with its upstream causes and clear directional relationships.

Use muted amber only for genuine risks, exceptions or constraints.

---

## 8.7 比较式｜模式比较 / 方案选择 / 前后变化 / 优劣分析

Use when two or more alternatives, states or approaches must be compared.

Align shared criteria and let visual weight reflect the actual conclusion. Balanced comparison may be symmetrical; directional comparison should emphasize the preferred or more important side when the source supports it.

Avoid decorative symmetry that obscures real differences.

---

## 8.8 能力体系式｜产品体系 / 服务体系 / 能力框架 / 运营体系

Use when several capabilities combine around one business objective, platform or service object.

Arrange capability groups according to dependency or business proximity. Place shared support capabilities in a subordinate common carrier when needed, and distinguish customer-facing outcomes clearly.

Avoid icon grids and repeated capability cards.

---

## 8.9 数据指标式｜规模 / 成效 / 市场 / 运营数据 / 关键指标

Use when numbers and evidence form the main argument.

Let one key metric, trend or quantitative judgment dominate, supported by a few relevant figures or evidence points.

Use restrained chart-like carriers when useful. Keep the page report-like rather than dashboard-like.

---

## 8.10 机制权责式｜运营机制 / 合作关系 / 边界 / 分工 / 治理

Use when the source focuses on responsibilities, control relationships, operating boundaries or collaboration mechanisms.

Build the page around the actual relationship axis or operating mechanism. Attach rights, responsibilities and boundaries directly to the relevant parties or relationships.

Avoid organization-chart aesthetics unless the content is genuinely hierarchical.

---

## 09｜跨区域支撑信息

Use a bottom or background support carrier only when genuine cross-cutting information applies to several major regions, such as shared rules, security constraints, common infrastructure or implementation conditions.

Keep it visually subordinate and structurally connected to the main body. Do not add a support band by default.

---

## 10｜图标与设备

Icons are optional and should be used only when they materially improve recognition. Keep them simple, small and subordinate to text, business objects and semantic visuals.

Do not let icons create independent modules, decorative rows or process sequences.

Screens and devices may appear only when they directly support the business meaning; do not add them by default.

---

## 11｜明确避免

Avoid visual patterns that weaken semantic clarity, especially:

- mechanical card walls, repeated equal modules or rigid grids without real equal-weight semantics;
- dashboard, SaaS, product-UI or interface-display aesthetics;
- decorative technology effects such as glossy 3D, glassmorphism, neon glow, floating icons, particles or excessive gradients;
- generic photography, abstract data landscapes or visual objects without a clear semantic role;
- excessive dark-blue filled boxes, thick borders or heavy framing.

Every major visual element must contribute to the page meaning and remain close to the information it supports.

---

# 12｜最终执行原则｜最高优先级

When rules conflict, follow these principles in order:

1. Preserve the source meaning, facts, boundaries and claim strength.
2. Derive the composition from the dominant semantic relationship.
3. Keep one dominant focus and one clear reading path.
4. Use visual materials only when they strengthen meaning or understanding.
5. Maintain the white + deep-blue, restrained, editorial executive-report visual language.
6. Prioritize readability and semantic clarity over any composition archetype, decorative device or stylistic habit.

Unify the visual language, not the page skeleton or visual-material type.

Final result: a calm, precise, highly readable and presentation-ready executive-report visual system that can support a complete leadership briefing deck without collapsing into repetitive templates.
