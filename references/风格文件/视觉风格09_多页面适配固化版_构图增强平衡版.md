# 视觉风格09：纯白 + 深蓝领导汇报｜多页面适配固化版

## 01｜色彩与基础气质

Palette: white #FFFFFF, deep blue #12355B, muted amber #D9772B, title #12355B, body #303030, secondary #6F7275, divider #C9CDD1.

Use deep blue for primary relationships, core judgments, key structural carriers and major conclusions. Reserve muted amber only for genuine risks, exceptions, constraints, pending status and occasional decision-critical emphasis; never use it as a category color or decorative accent.

Create a high-end senior leadership briefing page in a structured editorial business-infographic style: authoritative, calm, polished, precise and content-led. The page should feel like a refined executive-report infographic rather than a dashboard, poster, UI screen or icon-based diagram.

---

## 02｜固定视觉 DNA

Keep the visual language consistent across all page types while allowing the page skeleton and visual-material type to change with semantics.

Every page must preserve:

- pure-white background with restrained deep-blue hierarchy;
- one visually dominant focus and one clear reading path;
- one integrated composition rather than separate text and illustration zones;
- asymmetric and unequally weighted composition unless the source genuinely requires balanced comparison;
- concise Chinese presentation text with clear hierarchy;
- semantic visual materials integrated with the information structure when useful;
- precise alignment, thin pale-blue relationships and generous white space;
- few structural components, minimal decoration and strong editorial order.

Uniform visual language does not mean uniform page skeleton or uniform visual-material type.

---

## 03｜页面语义优先原则

Derive the page structure from the dominant semantic relationship in the source content.

Identify the primary relationship before composing:

- center-to-periphery → radial / hub-and-spoke;
- sequence or flow → main chain / process;
- time → timeline / roadmap;
- cause and effect → causal expansion;
- comparison → comparative structure;
- composition or dependency → main body + capability clusters;
- judgment and proof → conclusion + evidence;
- real-world business relationship → scene / object-led composition;
- quantitative argument → key metric + trend / evidence;
- governance or responsibility → relationship axis + boundary / support structure.

Do not force a familiar central, radial, ring-based, card-based or other template onto content whose semantics require another structure.

Use only the semantic regions required by the content. Prefer fewer, clearly differentiated and unequally weighted regions over repeated modules.

---

## 04｜文字与信息组织

Rewrite the supplied source copy into concise, conclusion-first Chinese presentation text.

Condense for presentation while preserving all material business meaning, including facts, numbers, conditions, scope, responsibilities, status and claim strength. Do not invent unsupported facts, slogans, conclusions or policy language.

Place text directly beside, above, below or partially within the related business object, visual material or structural carrier, so each topic forms one integrated local content unit.

Keep related text and visuals physically close. Do not place all explanatory text on one side and all visual material on another independent side.

Prefer short conclusion-first paragraphs, compact local explanations and bullets only when parallel items genuinely need distinction.

Do not reduce complete business meaning into isolated keywords merely to simplify the layout, and do not compress required body copy merely to create a visual center.

The page should remain understandable as a reading-type PPT without oral explanation.

---

## 05｜视觉对象与场景

Choose visual expression according to page semantics. Visual materials may include scenes, business objects, equipment, facilities, documents, resources, data graphics or structural relationships; realistic scenes are optional.

Derive visual subjects from the specific business objects, actors, actions, resources, environments and outcomes in the source content. Do not map broad content categories to fixed scene types.

Use scenes only when they materially strengthen business context or understanding. Otherwise let business objects, structure, text or data relationships carry the page.

Prefer several small or medium semantic visuals embedded within the information structure over one large decorative visual field, unless the page is explicitly visual-led.

Every visual element must support a specific topic, judgment, indicator, stakeholder, evidence point or business relationship and remain close to the information it supports.

Avoid generic power-industry imagery or multi-scene industry collages used only to signal “power industry”, “energy” or “technology”.

People are optional. If present, keep them non-portrait, visually subordinate and relevant to the business meaning.

---

## 06｜结构载体与空间层级

Every page must establish one strong semantic and visual anchor: a conclusion, key business object, metric, relationship, mechanism, stage, result, visual object or risk state.

Create hierarchy through scale, position, contrast, spacing and semantic centrality rather than additional boxes or decoration.

Maintain a visible order:

1. dominant focus;
2. major supporting directions, stages or evidence;
3. secondary objects, stakeholders or details;
4. cross-cutting conditions, constraints or support information.

Supporting information must remain subordinate while fully readable.

Use broad white fields and simple, restrained structural carriers only when they help organize the logic. Keep depth subtle and matte.

Do not let a large illustration or photographic field overpower the core judgment.

Avoid glossy 3D, sci-fi platforms, excessive perspective, floating objects or game-like visual effects.

---

## 07｜连接关系与精细度

Use deep-blue primary connectors and pale-blue secondary connectors. Keep relationships thin, sparse and precise.

Connectors must explain direction, dependency, flow, boundary or hierarchy rather than decorate empty space.

Use white space, precise alignment and restrained dividers to separate semantic layers instead of adding more boxes.

Text blocks, visual objects, connectors and structural carriers should align to a consistent editorial grid.

Avoid thick borders, heavy framing, dense line fields or decorative network effects.

---

# 08｜页面构图原型库

Select an archetype according to the dominant semantic relationship rather than the page title. These are compositional options, not templates.

Visual materials remain optional and must be derived from the source content.

## 8.1 中心放射式｜定位 / 架构 / 生态 / 多主体关系

Use only when the source has a genuine center-to-periphery relationship.

- core positioning, platform or principal object near the semantic center;
- major directions or mechanisms around it with unequal visual weight;
- stakeholders, outcomes or application domains farther outward as required.

Use restrained radial relationships and keep the core object dominant. Do not use this archetype when the content lacks a real center.

---

## 8.2 结论证据式｜背景 / 必要性 / 核心判断 / 价值说明

Use when one judgment is supported by several facts, business realities or quantitative proofs.

- one dominant conclusion region;
- supporting evidence arranged in fewer, unequally weighted regions;
- evidence placed close to the claim it supports.

Add visual or quantitative proof only where it strengthens the argument. Avoid equal evidence cards.

---

## 8.3 主链流程式｜业务流程 / 数据流通 / 实施机制 / 服务闭环

Use when the source has a clear sequence, flow or closed loop.

- one visible main chain;
- stages arranged according to actual sequence;
- critical nodes and final business result given stronger emphasis.

Embed business objects or small visuals into the chain where useful. Avoid rows of identical process boxes.

---

## 8.4 时间路线式｜阶段建设 / 路线图 / 年度计划 / 演进路径

Use when the sequence is explicitly temporal.

- one clear time carrier;
- stages weighted according to actual importance;
- milestones and deliverables close to their relevant stage;
- current or target stage emphasized when supported by the source.

Avoid generic equal-node timelines, oversized milestone icons and repeated circular badges.

---

## 8.5 场景 / 对象主导式｜应用场景 / 客户案例 / 业务方案 / 场景共建

Use when a business environment, key object or operating relationship is the most direct carrier of meaning.

- one dominant scene, object or operating relationship as the main carrier;
- actors, inputs, actions and outputs annotated directly around or within it;
- supporting text integrated with the relevant part of the carrier.

The visual carrier becomes the page skeleton rather than an illustration placed beside the text. Do not surround it with unrelated cards.

---

## 8.6 因果分析式｜问题 / 原因 / 约束 / 风险传导

Use when the source explains why an issue occurs or how conditions lead to an outcome.

- one dominant problem, consequence or constraint;
- upstream causes arranged according to actual causal logic;
- directional relationships made explicit.

Use muted amber only for genuine risks, exceptions or constraints; never merely to differentiate categories.

---

## 8.7 比较式｜模式比较 / 方案选择 / 前后变化 / 优劣分析

Use when alternatives, states or approaches must be compared.

- align shared comparison criteria;
- let visual weight reflect the actual conclusion;
- emphasize meaningful differences through scale, placement or evidence.

Balanced comparison may be symmetrical when genuinely balanced. Directional comparison should remain unequal when the source supports a preferred or more important side.

---

## 8.8 能力体系式｜产品体系 / 服务体系 / 能力框架 / 运营体系

Use when several capabilities combine around one business objective, platform or service object.

- one main business objective, platform or service object;
- capability groups arranged by dependency, business proximity or service logic;
- shared support capabilities kept subordinate and customer-facing outcomes clearly distinguished.

Use text, business objects or structural grouping rather than repeated capability cards. Avoid icon grids.

---

## 8.9 数据指标式｜规模 / 成效 / 市场 / 运营数据 / 关键指标

Use when numbers and evidence form the main argument.

- one dominant key metric, trend or quantitative judgment;
- supporting figures placed close to the relevant conclusion;
- restrained chart-like or proportional carriers when useful.

Contextual visuals remain secondary. Keep the page editorial and report-like rather than dashboard-like.

---

## 8.10 机制权责式｜运营机制 / 合作关系 / 边界 / 分工 / 治理

Use when the source focuses on responsibilities, control relationships, operating boundaries or collaboration mechanisms.

- one clear relationship axis or operating mechanism;
- parties positioned according to actual relationship rather than visual symmetry;
- rights, responsibilities and boundaries attached directly to the relevant relationship.

Use precise connectors and restrained boundary lines. Avoid organization-chart aesthetics unless the content is genuinely hierarchical.

---

## 09｜跨区域支撑信息

Use a bottom or background support carrier only when genuine cross-cutting information applies to several major regions, such as shared rules, security constraints, common infrastructure, implementation conditions or ownership boundaries.

Keep it visually subordinate and structurally connected to the main body. Do not add a support band merely to fill the bottom of the page.

---

## 10｜图标与设备

Icons are optional and should be used only when they materially improve recognition. Keep them simple, small and subordinate to text, business objects and semantic visuals.

Do not let icons create independent modules, decorative rows, process sequences, repeated circular badges or the main page skeleton.

Screens and devices may appear only when they directly support the business meaning; do not add them by default.

Avoid icon-led timelines and oversized icon nodes.

---

## 11｜明确避免

Avoid composition patterns that weaken semantic clarity or turn the page into a generic PPT template, especially:

- mechanical card walls or repeated equal modules without genuine equal-weight semantics;
- default left-text / right-image compositions;
- separating text and visual material into two independent halves;
- giant decorative hero illustrations or oversized photographic fields;
- panoramic industry collages assembled from unrelated scenes;
- repeated circular badges, oversized icons or icon-led timelines;
- dashboard, SaaS, product-UI or interface-display aesthetics;
- mechanical three-column layouts used only for convenience;
- glossy 3D, glassmorphism, neon glow, floating icons, particles or excessive gradients;
- abstract data landscapes or dense flowing-line fields without business meaning;
- excessive dark-blue filled boxes, thick borders or heavy framing;
- isolated decorative photography or visual objects without a clear semantic role.

Do not treat every content item as an independent rectangle.

Every major visual element must contribute to the page meaning and remain close to the information it supports.

---

# 12｜最终执行原则｜最高优先级

When rules conflict, follow these principles in order:

1. Preserve the source meaning, facts, boundaries and claim strength.
2. Derive the composition from the dominant semantic relationship.
3. Build one integrated composition with one dominant focus and one clear reading path.
4. Keep related text, visual objects and evidence physically integrated rather than split into independent text and image zones.
5. Use visual materials only when they strengthen meaning; visual-material type is determined by the source, not by the style preset.
6. Maintain the white + deep-blue, asymmetric, restrained and editorial executive-report visual language.
7. Prioritize readability, semantic clarity and spatial hierarchy over any composition archetype or decorative device.

Unify the visual language, not the page skeleton or visual-material type.

Freedom in what to visualize; discipline in how the page is organized.

Final result: a calm, precise, highly readable and presentation-ready executive-report visual system that can support a complete leadership briefing deck without collapsing into repetitive templates.
