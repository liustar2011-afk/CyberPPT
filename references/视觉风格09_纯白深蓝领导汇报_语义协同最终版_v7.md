# 视觉风格09：纯白 + 深蓝领导汇报｜语义协同最终版

## 01｜色彩与基础气质

Palette: white #FFFFFF, deep blue #12355B, muted amber #D9772B, title #12355B, body #303030, secondary #6F7275, divider #C9CDD1.

Use deep blue for primary relationships, core judgments, key structural carriers and major conclusions. Reserve muted amber only for genuine risks, exceptions, constraints, pending status and occasional decision-critical emphasis; never use it as a category color or decorative accent.

Create a high-end senior leadership briefing page in a structured editorial business-infographic style: authoritative, calm, polished, precise, content-led and presentation-ready.

The page should feel like a refined executive-report composition rather than a dashboard, poster, UI screen, icon sheet or generic infographic template.

The visual tone should be restrained but not stiff, sophisticated but not decorative, information-rich but not crowded.

---

## 02｜固定视觉 DNA

The visual language must remain consistent across all page types, while the page skeleton may change intelligently according to semantics.

Every page must preserve the following visual DNA:

- pure-white background with restrained deep-blue hierarchy;
- one visually dominant focus;
- realistic electric-power or business scenes integrated with information structure;
- asymmetric and unequally weighted composition;
- concise Chinese presentation text with clear hierarchy;
- thin dividers, precise alignment and refined pale-blue relationship lines;
- generous white space and strong reading order;
- few structural components and few decorative elements;
- editorial, scene-supported and content-led rather than dashboard-like;
- icons strictly subordinate to text, structure, data and real-world scenes.

The style must remain recognizable even when the composition changes from radial to sequential, comparative, temporal, causal, quantitative, scene-led or governance-oriented.

Unify the visual language, not the page skeleton.

---

## 03｜语义理解与适度内容展开｜最高优先级

Treat the supplied source as the primary factual and semantic foundation, not as a rigid sentence-by-sentence boundary and not as the maximum amount of content allowed on the page.

Understand the source first. Then rewrite, regroup, condense and, when useful, moderately expand it to improve logic, executive readability and visual expression.

The model should actively reason about what the source means rather than mechanically preserving sentence order or paragraph structure.

### 3.1 三层内容处理

Use three levels of content transformation:

**Level A — Source facts and explicit claims**
Preserve important business objects, numbers, dates, scope, responsibility, conditions, status and claim strength accurately.

**Level B — Semantic inference and editorial synthesis**
Encouraged. Make implicit relationships explicit, elevate a supported higher-level judgment, merge related statements, reveal causal or dependency logic, and write concise conclusion-first presentation language.

**Level C — Moderate domain enrichment**
Allowed when useful. Add small amounts of low-risk, domain-common explanation, transition, implication or contextual framing when it helps the page feel complete and independently understandable.

The page should remain anchored in the source. Expansion should strengthen the source's argument rather than compete with it.

### 3.2 扩展边界

Moderate enrichment may include:

- concise summary judgments;
- explicit logical bridges;
- natural consequence statements strongly implied by the source;
- short explanation of why a fact matters;
- familiar domain terminology that clarifies an existing idea;
- a limited amount of executive framing that improves continuity or readability.

Be conservative with unsupported precision.

Avoid introducing highly specific new numbers, dates, formal policy wording, named responsibilities, technical parameters, implementation commitments or project status as established facts unless they are adequately supported.

Do not expand one source point into a complete multi-part framework merely because such a framework is familiar or visually convenient.

Do not add content merely to fill empty space.

### 3.3 展开方向

Expand vertically along the page's main argument before expanding horizontally into new topics.

Added content should preferably:

- sharpen the dominant judgment;
- deepen an existing evidence point;
- clarify an existing stage, cause, dependency, boundary or outcome;
- make an implicit relationship visible;
- improve the transition between existing semantic regions.

Prefer enriching an existing region before creating a new region.

A new region is justified only when it contributes a genuinely different semantic function to the page.

One page should read as one coherent argument, not as several plausible analyses placed together.

---

## 04｜构图前的语义建模｜最高优先级

Before composing the page, silently reconstruct the source as a semantic model rather than as sentence chunks.

Internally determine:

1. the single dominant judgment or page mission;
2. the primary semantic relationship;
3. the supporting semantic roles;
4. the hierarchy and dependency among those roles;
5. which information deserves visual emphasis;
6. which information can be compressed or omitted;
7. where limited enrichment would materially improve understanding;
8. which visual carrier should express each important semantic unit.

Do not display this analysis as meta-commentary on the page.

### 4.1 常见语义角色

Statements may function as:

- dominant judgment;
- background / driver;
- factual basis / evidence;
- object / resource;
- action / requirement;
- mechanism / path;
- relationship / dependency;
- stage / milestone;
- outcome / value;
- boundary / condition;
- risk / constraint;
- stakeholder / responsibility;
- quantitative proof.

Rebuild the page according to semantic role and importance rather than original sentence order.

### 4.2 主关系判断

Identify the dominant semantic relationship before selecting a composition:

- center-to-periphery → radial / hub-and-spoke;
- sequential → main chain / process;
- temporal → timeline / roadmap;
- causal → cause-and-effect;
- comparative → asymmetric comparison;
- compositional → main body + clusters;
- evidence-based → conclusion + evidence;
- real-world business → scene-led composition;
- quantitative → key metric + trend / proof;
- governance / responsibility → relationship axis + boundary / support carrier.

Secondary relationships may appear locally, but they must remain subordinate to the primary relationship.

Do not build two or three full narrative frameworks on the same page.

### 4.3 并列性判断

Use parallel modules only when the items are genuinely parallel.

Parallel items should normally:

- answer the same question;
- belong to the same abstraction level;
- perform the same logical role;
- have comparable semantic weight.

If several statements play different roles, express them through hierarchy, sequence, causality, nesting, evidence linkage, annotation or spatial dependency instead of forcing them into equal sibling blocks.

Never turn a paragraph into a row of cards merely because it contains several clauses.

---

## 05｜文字与信息组织

Rewrite the supplied source into concise, conclusion-first Chinese presentation text.

The page should remain understandable without oral explanation, but independent readability does not require reproducing the full prose source.

### 5.1 文字职责

Use text primarily for:

- judgment;
- interpretation;
- boundary and condition;
- explanation that cannot be efficiently encoded visually;
- concise evidence statements;
- key labels and transitions.

Prefer:

- one dominant conclusion;
- compact local explanations;
- short factual statements;
- short bullets only when items are truly parallel;
- meaningful phrases rather than isolated keywords;
- clear local grouping around the related object, scene or relationship.

Do not preserve sentence order when a different order creates a clearer argument.

Do not create a heading for every clause.

Do not split one coherent idea into several independent text boxes simply to make the page look structured.

### 5.2 文字密度

Use as much text as the logic requires, but no more.

Avoid both extremes:

- dense paragraphs that overwhelm the visual structure;
- over-compression into vague labels that lose business meaning.

Supporting text should usually be shorter than the main judgment and should remain visually subordinate.

Use hierarchy, proximity, alignment, scale and visual carriers to reduce explanatory burden instead of adding more words.

### 5.3 语义去重

Do not restate the same meaning in multiple regions under different labels.

A conclusion already established in the dominant region should not reappear as separate “意义、价值、总体判断、核心作用、总结” modules unless the new region contributes materially different information.

One idea should normally have one primary visual home.

If two regions largely repeat each other, merge them and use the released space for hierarchy, scene integration or white space.

---

## 06｜图文分工与语义载体｜最高优先级

Text, structure, quantitative graphics, realistic scenes and icons must perform different semantic jobs.

Do not use multiple carriers to fully restate the same information.

For every important semantic unit, assign one primary carrier.

### 6.1 Primary carrier rules

**Text**
Best for judgment, explanation, condition, boundary, nuance and conclusion.

**Structural graphics**
Best for relationship, sequence, hierarchy, causality, scope, dependency, flow and composition.

**Quantitative graphics / large numerals**
Best for scale, comparison, proportion, change, trend and measurable evidence.

**Realistic scenes**
Best for real-world context, business environment, operating state, stakeholder setting, scale, evidence and situational meaning.

**Icons**
Best only for recognition, navigation and light labeling.

### 6.2 一个观点，一个主要表达归宿

One idea should normally have one primary visual home.

Once a semantic unit has a clear primary carrier, secondary carriers may only:

- label it;
- clarify one aspect;
- provide context or evidence;
- reinforce recognition;
- connect it to another semantic unit.

They should not reproduce the full meaning again.

Do not write a paragraph, draw a diagram, add an icon and place a photo all to say exactly the same thing.

### 6.3 图文互补原则

Text should not narrate what the structure already makes obvious.

If a relationship is clearly shown through flow, hierarchy or spatial structure, reduce the adjacent text to the judgment, implication, key condition or short label.

If text carries the nuanced judgment, the visual should add context, evidence, relationship, scale or operating reality rather than merely illustrating the noun.

If a number is already visually dominant, do not repeat the same number in nearby body copy unless interpretation is needed.

If a scene adds no meaning beyond decoration, reduce or remove it.

### 6.4 Complementarity test

Before finalizing each local text-visual unit, silently check:

- What unique meaning is carried by the text?
- What unique meaning is carried by the visual?
- If one is removed, what information is actually lost?

If the answer is “almost nothing,” simplify one of them.

Keep both only when each contributes distinct information.

Deliberate repetition is allowed only for the single dominant conclusion or a decision-critical point, and even then should be restrained.

---

## 07｜实景与结构融合

Prefer a scene-supported executive-report language.

Use realistic, clean and context-rich electric-power and business scenes as visual anchors, such as:

- grid facilities;
- substations;
- dispatch centers;
- control rooms;
- transmission lines;
- wind and solar bases;
- energy storage equipment;
- power plants;
- industrial facilities;
- professional analysis environments;
- teaching and research environments;
- operational resources;
- business collaboration environments.

### 7.1 场景必须增加语义

A scene should not merely illustrate a noun already written in text.

Prefer scenes that add one or more of the following:

- real-world context;
- operating state;
- stakeholder environment;
- spatial relationship;
- scale;
- evidence;
- contrast;
- consequence;
- business realism.

Do not assign one photo to every bullet point.

Use the minimum number of scenes that materially improve the page.

One dominant scene is appropriate when the real-world environment itself carries the argument. Several smaller scenes are appropriate only when they represent genuinely different contexts or evidence.

### 7.2 场景与信息结构融合

Integrate scenes through:

- soft edge fading;
- cropped photographic windows;
- partial background embedding;
- shared boundaries with text regions;
- scene-object overlap with nearby structural elements;
- white-to-image gradient transitions;
- selective foreground / background layering.

The scene should feel fused with the information architecture rather than pasted into a card.

Do not place every scene inside a rigid frame.

People should be absent or minimal. If present, keep them small, non-portrait and subordinate to the environment.

---

## 08｜结构载体、空间层级与编辑张力

Every page must establish one strong semantic and visual anchor.

The dominant focus may be:

- a core conclusion;
- a platform or business object;
- a major metric;
- a realistic scene;
- a critical relationship;
- a principal mechanism;
- a stage or turning point;
- a risk or constraint state.

Express it through scale, position, contrast, deep blue, limited muted amber, scene scale, structural carrier or semantic centrality.

### 8.1 空间层级

Maintain visible hierarchy:

1. dominant focus;
2. major evidence / stages / directions;
3. secondary objects / annotations / details;
4. cross-cutting conditions / constraints / support.

Supporting information must remain subordinate while still readable.

### 8.2 编辑式张力

Style 09 should be calm, but not visually safe or lifeless.

Create controlled tension through:

- large versus small regions;
- dense versus sparse areas;
- scene versus white space;
- dominant anchor versus secondary evidence;
- straight versus curved structural carriers;
- concentrated information versus open breathing room.

The page should feel designed, not merely arranged.

Avoid defaulting to a mechanically complete top-middle-bottom stack.

Avoid defaulting to large text on one side and a separate image on the other when stronger integration is possible.

Allow the dominant scene, key relationship and conclusion to interlock when semantics support it.

Use overlap, fading, shared carriers and asymmetric spacing selectively to create one integrated composition.

### 8.3 留白

White space is an active structural element.

Use it to create rhythm, hierarchy and separation.

Do not fill unused space with extra content, icons or decorative shapes merely because the canvas appears open.

---

## 09｜连接关系与精细度

Use deep-blue primary connectors and pale-blue secondary connectors.

Favor:

- thin precise lines;
- large-radius arcs;
- short arrows;
- restrained dotted relationships;
- subtle circular guides;
- clean directional paths.

Connectors must explain business logic rather than decorate empty space.

Use connectors only when they clarify:

- direction;
- dependency;
- sequence;
- interaction;
- scope;
- evidence linkage.

Maintain generous white space around key text and structural junctions.

Use visual breathing room to separate semantic layers instead of adding more boxes.

Use thin dividers, precise alignment, controlled spacing and consistent corner geometry.

Text blocks, scene edges, connectors and structural carriers should align to an invisible editorial grid.

Avoid thick borders and heavy framing.

Where a region needs definition, prefer:

- a very light blue-gray outline;
- a thin divider;
- a broad white field;
- a softly faded scene boundary.

# 10｜页面构图原型库

The following composition archetypes are spatial grammars, not mandatory templates.

Select one primary archetype according to the dominant semantic relationship.

Secondary motifs may be embedded locally, but must remain subordinate and must not become additional full-page narratives.

Suggested region counts are guidance, not quotas.

Do not generate content simply to fill an archetype.

## 10.1 中心放射式｜定位 / 架构 / 生态 / 多主体关系

Use when the source expresses a genuine center-to-periphery relationship.

Preferred structure:

- center or upper-center = core positioning / platform / principal object;
- middle layer = major directions, mechanisms or capabilities;
- outer layer = stakeholders, service objects, outcomes or application domains;
- optional support carrier = cross-cutting conditions or shared infrastructure.

Use pale-blue arcs, restrained radial relationships and a strong central semantic anchor.

Do not use radial composition when no true semantic center exists.

---

## 10.2 结论证据式｜背景 / 必要性 / 核心判断 / 价值说明

Use when one judgment dominates and several facts, conditions or realities support it.

Preferred structure:

- one dominant conclusion;
- several unequal supporting evidence regions;
- selective scene or quantitative evidence;
- optional implication or outcome.

The page should feel more spacious than an architecture page.

Avoid equal evidence cards.

---

## 10.3 主链流程式｜业务流程 / 数据流通 / 实施机制 / 服务闭环

Use when the source has a clear sequence, movement or transformation.

Preferred structure:

- one visible main chain;
- several meaningful stages of unequal weight;
- larger emphasis only on critical stages;
- stronger terminal outcome or business result when supported.

Embed business objects, evidence and scenes along the chain rather than using identical process boxes.

Arrows should be directional, sparse and precise.

---

## 10.4 时间路线式｜阶段建设 / 路线图 / 年度计划 / 演进路径

Use when sequence is explicitly temporal.

Preferred structure:

- one clear time carrier;
- unequal stages or milestones;
- current, turning or target stage emphasized;
- deliverables and implications attached close to their relevant point in time.

Do not render a generic equal-node timeline.

Use spacing, scale and scene emphasis to express phase importance.

---

## 10.5 场景主导式｜应用场景 / 客户案例 / 业务方案 / 场景共建

Use when the real-world operating environment itself is the strongest carrier of meaning.

Preferred structure:

- one dominant realistic scene as the page skeleton;
- actors, inputs, actions, data or outputs annotated directly around the real business relationship;
- few connectors;
- one concise conclusion or outcome region.

Do not place a scene in the middle and surround it with unrelated cards.

Annotations must attach to real objects, actions or relationships.

---

## 10.6 因果分析式｜问题 / 原因 / 约束 / 风险传导

Use when the source explains why something occurs or how conditions lead to an outcome.

Preferred structure:

- one dominant consequence, problem or target state;
- several upstream causes or conditions;
- clear directional relationships;
- optional resulting impact, response or boundary condition.

Use muted amber only for genuine risks, exceptions or constraints.

---

## 10.7 不对称比较式｜模式比较 / 方案选择 / 前后变化 / 优劣分析

Use when alternatives, states or modes must be contrasted.

Preferred structure:

- unequal comparison zones;
- aligned criteria where comparison is meaningful;
- one dominant difference or recommendation when supported;
- scenes only where they add real comparative meaning.

Avoid symmetrical side-by-side cards when the conclusion is not balanced.

---

## 10.8 能力体系式｜产品体系 / 服务体系 / 能力框架 / 运营体系

Use when several capabilities combine around one business objective or service object.

Preferred structure:

- one main business objective / platform / service object;
- capability clusters arranged by dependency or business proximity;
- shared infrastructure or support placed as a background / bottom carrier when genuinely cross-cutting;
- customer-facing outcome shown distinctly.

Avoid icon grids.

Capabilities should be represented through text, business objects, structural grouping and selective scenes.

---

## 10.9 数据指标式｜规模 / 成效 / 市场 / 运营数据 / 关键指标

Use when numbers and evidence are the main argument.

Preferred structure:

- one dominant metric, trend or quantitative judgment;
- a small number of supporting figures or comparisons;
- restrained chart-like carriers when useful;
- realistic scene only as contextual evidence.

The page should remain editorial and report-like, not become a dashboard.

Avoid collections of KPI cards.

---

## 10.10 机制权责式｜运营机制 / 合作关系 / 边界 / 分工 / 治理

Use when the source focuses on responsibilities, control relationships, operating boundaries or collaboration mechanisms.

Preferred structure:

- one central relationship axis or operating mechanism;
- parties positioned according to actual relationship rather than visual symmetry;
- rights, responsibilities, boundaries and retained ownership attached directly to relevant relationships;
- optional support carrier for shared implementation rules or governance capabilities.

Use precise connectors and restrained boundary lines.

Avoid organization-chart aesthetics unless the content is genuinely hierarchical.

---

## 11｜底部协同带规则

A bottom horizontal band may be used only when the page contains genuinely cross-cutting information that supports the main body, such as:

- existing-system linkage;
- implementation modes;
- governance rules;
- common technical support;
- ownership boundaries;
- security constraints;
- shared infrastructure.

The bottom band should read as a structural carrier, not as an additional card wall or mandatory summary zone.

It may use:

- thin dividers;
- sparse labels;
- very small monochrome symbols;
- one or two concise text groups;
- a light connection back to the main body.

Do not create a bottom band simply because the lower area is empty.

Do not synthesize generic “技术、标准、安全、运营、政策、机制、保障”等 support systems merely to make the page look complete.

---

## 12｜图标与设备约束

Icons are recognition aids, not primary semantic carriers.

Use icons only when they materially improve recognition or navigation.

Do not convert every text point into an icon-text pair.

If text or structure already makes the concept clear, omit the icon.

Keep icons:

- simple;
- deep blue;
- small;
- integrated into an existing text or structural region.

Do not give icons:

- independent cards;
- circular badges by default;
- decorative containers;
- dedicated display zones;
- equal visual weight with real business objects or scenes.

Screens and devices may appear only as supporting evidence inside a broader operating environment.

Avoid:

- repeated devices;
- isolated UI screenshots;
- dashboard mockups;
- SaaS interface aesthetics;
- product-display layouts.

---

## 13｜明确避免

Avoid:

- equal card walls;
- equal rows or columns used as the default solution;
- mechanical three-column layouts;
- habitual left-text / right-image compositions;
- one paragraph = one module;
- one clause = one icon + one sentence;
- panoramic poster aesthetics;
- giant decorative hero illustrations without semantic function;
- abstract data landscapes;
- dense flowing-line fields;
- icon grids;
- repeated circular badges;
- glossy 3D;
- glassmorphism;
- neon glow;
- floating icons;
- decorative technology particles;
- excessive gradients;
- dashboard tiles;
- product-UI aesthetics.

Also avoid:

- mechanically converting prose into sibling cards;
- making non-parallel statements visually parallel;
- preserving sentence order when it weakens the logic;
- stacking several independent narrative frameworks on one page;
- repeating the same conclusion as background, value, significance and summary;
- adding a new section when a short annotation can complete the logic;
- using content expansion to fill visual space;
- using a photo merely to repeat a noun already stated in text;
- using a diagram merely to redraw a sentence that the text already explains completely;
- using text merely to narrate what a diagram already makes obvious;
- treating every available visual carrier as mandatory.

---

# 14｜最终视觉执行约束｜最高优先级

Every page must establish one visually dominant focus that makes the core judgment immediately recognizable.

The page must have:

- one clear page mission;
- one dominant semantic relationship;
- one primary narrative framework;
- one strong reading path;
- strong spatial hierarchy;
- differentiated semantic carriers;
- complementary rather than repetitive text and visuals;
- selective, meaningful real-world scenes;
- precise editorial alignment;
- thin and purposeful connectors;
- generous white space;
- restrained visual depth;
- controlled editorial tension.

Use model knowledge to clarify and moderately enrich the source, not to overwhelm it.

Semantic inference, editorial synthesis and limited domain enrichment are encouraged.

Unsupported precision, false certainty, redundant frameworks and mechanical content completion are not.

### Final semantic check

Before finalizing the page, silently verify:

1. Is the dominant judgment immediately clear?
2. Does the page follow one main argument?
3. Are parallel items genuinely parallel?
4. Has moderate enrichment strengthened rather than broadened the argument?
5. Does each important idea have one primary visual home?
6. Do text and visuals contribute different information?
7. Does every scene add context, evidence, scale or operating meaning?
8. Is any region repeating another region under a different label?
9. Can any text, icon, box, scene or connector be removed without losing meaning?
10. Is unused space functioning as hierarchy rather than being unnecessarily filled?

If a component fails these checks, simplify, merge, reposition or remove it.

### Style 09 的最终统一原则

Unify the visual language, not the page skeleton.

Across a complete presentation, Style 09 should remain recognizable through:

- white + deep-blue executive-report aesthetics;
- one dominant focus per page;
- strong semantic logic;
- moderate but controlled content expansion;
- realistic contextual electric-power scenes;
- differentiated semantic carriers;
- complementary text-and-visual expression;
- refined pale-blue structural relationships;
- restrained matte depth;
- asymmetric editorial composition;
- high readability;
- minimal decorative graphics.

Final result: a calm, logical, visually refined and presentation-ready executive-report system in which content is understood before it is laid out, moderate enrichment improves meaning, and every text or visual element contributes distinct semantic value rather than repeating the same information in another form.
