## p01

【完整上屏内容】
平台总体定位为电力数据要素流通共享的行业中枢和价值释放引擎，由中心定位表述、三重建设方向和四类服务对象构成放射状结构：三重方向并列环绕中心定位展开，四类服务对象居于最外层，体现服务范围逐层拓展的逻辑关系。
三重建设方向并列展开：一是建设国家数据基础设施电力行业节点，承担电力行业侧主体连接、目录衔接、接口协同、可信流通和场景承接功能，逐步衔接全域功能节点、区域节点和其他行业节点；二是建设行业数据与专业能力运营平台，统一组织数据资源、模型能力、专业能力和技术能力，衔接资源登记、产品管理、客户服务、可信交付和运营评价；三是建设多主体协同和价值共创载体，组织需求单位、资源方、模型算法方、专业服务方和技术实施方，围绕行业共性需求开展产品开发、场景共建和持续运营。
四类服务对象居于最外层，由三重方向共同支撑：一是面向政府部门，为宏观能源决策、能源规划和行业治理提供数据分析支撑；二是面向新型电力系统建设，提供行业数据底座；三是面向电力企业，为生产经营、科技创新和数字化转型提供专业服务；四是面向产业链上下游及跨行业主体，为协同创新创造基础条件。
平台与各单位既有业务系统、数据平台、模型平台和安全体系协同衔接，承担跨主体连接、共性规则执行、产品运营和可信服务协同，平台接入不改变合作伙伴对原始数据、核心模型、关键算法和既有知识产权的控制关系，并根据资源属性和业务需要支持集中服务、源端处理、专属环境和受控计算等多种实施方式。









【输出尺寸｜不上屏】
最高优先级画布约束：输出必须严格为 2048×1024 像素（2:1）的正文内容区图片；不得输出16:9完整幻灯片。输入参考图只用于视觉风格与构图语言，不得继承参考图的画布比例。不得绘制页面标题、副标题、页码、页面序号、Logo 或页脚；标题/副标题由 PPT 模板文字层承载。



【视觉风格｜不上屏】
视觉风格09：纯白 + 深蓝领导汇报

Palette: white #FFFFFF, deep blue #12355B, muted amber #D9772B, title #12355B, body #303030, secondary #6F7275, divider #C9CDD1. Use deep blue for primary relationships and conclusions. Reserve muted amber only for risks, exceptions, constraints, pending status, and occasional decision-critical emphasis; never use it as a category color or decorative accent.

Create a high-end senior leadership briefing page in a structured editorial business-infographic style: authoritative, calm, polished and content-led.

Build one integrated, asymmetric and unequally weighted composition around a single semantic carrier. The carrier must own the page geometry, dominant judgment and reading path. Supporting content may attach to, orbit, branch from or sit beneath this carrier, while remaining part of the same continuous composition.

Use at most three primary content regions on a standard page. Repeated peers must share one continuous field, band, orbit, network or grouped structure. Do not place each peer in its own bordered card. Do not turn headings into navigation rails or independent display blocks.

Rewrite the supplied source copy into concise, conclusion-first Chinese presentation text. Place the rewritten text directly beside, above, below or partially within the related business object or scene, so text and visual material form one local content unit. Preserve business objects, facts, numbers, conditions, scope, responsibility, status and claim strength; do not invent unsupported facts or slogans.

Choose one dominant visual medium per page: relationship diagram, one contextual business scene, object illustration or data visualization. Use a second medium only when it proves a different source-backed claim. Do not assign one photo or one icon to every item.

For relationship-led pages, prioritize the relationship diagram over decorative scenes. Show both ends of every claimed relationship and the connecting action. When the source declares a center, surrounding layers, shared support, mapping, flow or radial expansion, preserve those relations in one continuous geometry.

Use realistic, clean and context-rich business scenes only when they materially prove or contextualize a source-backed claim. Use no more than two photographs on a standard page. Keep scenes subordinate to the semantic carrier and avoid photo-per-item compositions.

Use icons only when they materially improve recognition. Use no more than two semantic icons on a standard page. Keep them small, simple, deep blue and embedded within an existing text, scene or business region, with only barely perceptible tonal relief.

Do not give icons independent cards, circular badges, decorative containers or display areas. Icons must not determine the composition, create extra modules, form icon rows or process sequences, or replace scenes, business objects or source-backed text.

Screens and devices may appear only as supporting evidence inside a broader working scene. Avoid repeated devices, isolated UI screenshots, dashboards, SaaS interfaces and product-display layouts.

Never invent examples, implementation details, technology names, operational claims or explanatory captions. Every visible factual phrase must be a faithful rewrite of supplied source text. If the source does not provide a detail, leave it unstated.

When all supplied copy cannot remain readable at presentation size, preserve the core judgment, named business objects, relationships, responsibilities, conditions and boundaries first. Merge repeated wording and omit decorative labels or images. Do not solve overload by shrinking body text or multiplying modules.

Use broad flat fields and pure-white content regions, deep-blue headings, thin dividers, restrained connectors, matte materials and very shallow depth.

Avoid equal card walls, equal rows or columns, left-side section rails, dashboard bands, repeated bordered containers, icon inventories, photo-per-item layouts, template-like three-row stacks, left-text / center-image / right-text layouts, panoramic posters, giant hero illustrations, abstract data landscapes, dense flowing-line fields, icon grids, glossy 3D, glassmorphism, neon glow and floating icons.

People should be absent or minimal. Reference images may inform palette, spacing, scene mood and polish only.

The reference image controls palette, material, whitespace, lighting and photographic tone only. Never copy its layout, object positions, lines or spatial proportions. It cannot authorize a structure forbidden by this contract.

Final result: a calm, highly readable executive-report page with one unmistakable semantic carrier, strong relationship visibility, few components, restrained contextual imagery and strictly subordinate icons.

【最终视觉执行约束｜最高优先级】
Every page must establish one visually dominant focus that makes the core judgment immediately recognizable. Express it through scale, position, contrast, deep blue or limited muted amber emphasis, or a semantic anchor. The focus may be a conclusion, key metric, business object, scene, critical relationship, or risk state. Supporting information must remain subordinate. Do not add unrelated decorative objects or compress required body copy merely to create a focus.
