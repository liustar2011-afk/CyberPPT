## p01

Create a polished, high-end enterprise architecture infographic for a Chinese power-industry data infrastructure platform. Use a 2:1 horizontal canvas (2048 × 1024), deep navy and electric cyan accents on a clean warm-white background, restrained premium vector style, sharp Chinese typography, thin precise connector lines, very subtle abstract power-grid background only. This is one integrated architecture diagram: all meaningful text must live physically inside architecture layers, guard rails, endpoint nodes, or along the process connector. The text itself is the diagram.

Composition: a large central platform occupies most of the canvas. Build a vertical stack of five broad, connected horizontal capability layers in the middle, with clear ascending structure and one unified platform silhouette. Put the following Chinese labels inside the corresponding layers, keeping every phrase legible and visually embedded:

Top layer: “可信连接与主体接入” with “可信身份｜主体接入｜业务请求”.
Second layer: “运营与交易管理” with “订单合同｜授权计量｜账单结算”.
Third layer: “服务交付与场景编排” with “接口调用｜数据沙箱｜模型任务”.
Fourth layer: “资源与产品管理” with “登记编目｜质量版本｜可订购产品”.
Bottom layer: “数据与人工智能基础” with “存储计算｜知识库｜模型服务”.

Across the core stack, use a single prominent thin cyan process connector with labeled arrow segments: “经授权资源 → 可订购产品 → 受控服务 → 计量留痕”. The arrow must visibly connect the resource/product, service delivery, and operation layers. Add a small central junction label “权利核查与授权规则” that visibly links resource management, service delivery, and operation management.

Create two separate narrow vertical guard rails running alongside the entire five-layer stack. On the left, label “安全与合规” and embed “分类分级｜权利核查｜访问控制｜输出管理｜审计追溯”. On the right, label “标准与运维” and embed “目录接口｜质量服务等级｜版本监控｜容灾处置”. They must clearly cover all five layers rather than appear as a separate bottom section.

From the top trusted-connection layer, draw four distinct connector lines to compact external endpoint nodes around the platform. Place the endpoint text inside the nodes: “统一环境：公共能力”, “源端服务：合作伙伴资源”, “专属实例：客户专属能力”, and “受控计算：多方敏感数据联合分析”. The endpoints should sit around the central platform as connected environments, never as an independent deployment block.

At the very top of the diagram, use only one compact in-diagram conclusion label: “可信连接组织资源产品化与受控服务”. No separate explanatory paragraphs. No left text column. No right-side photo or illustration panel. No photo collage, people, cards filled with body copy, detached captions, independent bottom strip, logos, slide title, subtitle, or page number. Use only small abstract vector icons within the corresponding nodes where helpful. The central architecture diagram must occupy the visual focus and communicate the complete story without any detached text area.
